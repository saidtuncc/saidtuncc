### Now: Atölye Defteri
> Customer, vehicle, work-order and payment management for auto repair shops, with a live tracking page for car owners.

- **8** businesses · **~45** daily users · **1000+** work orders
- **Defterci:** an AI assistant with 17 tools. It reads the shop's records, summarises the day and drafts customer messages; every change it suggests needs a human's approval.
- Multi-tenant on a single server, with automated backups, self-healing monitoring and staged releases
- **4,000+** automated tests

→ [How it's built](https://github.com/saidtuncc/atolye-defteri) · [Live demo](https://demo.atolyedefteri.com)
